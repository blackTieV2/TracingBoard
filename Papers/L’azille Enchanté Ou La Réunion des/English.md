## The Enchanted Azille or The Reunion of the Two Sexes Apprentice Grade 

### Chamber of Reflection

This chamber must be adorned in white with a table in the middle, covered with a white cloth, two armchairs, one in front of the table and the other beside it. The Initiate is seated within without a chair, facing the table. The second armchair is used on days when the illustrious Master or Warden is present there. The table must include a vanity mirror and a writing desk with a single lit lamp in the said chamber.

---

### Preparation of the Initiate

The Sister Deputy of the Very Illustrious Master examines and asks the Lady or Young Woman if she harbors any critical doubts, such as pregnancy or other conditions, and verifies whether she is in a state to endure the trials or requires alternate accommodations. She ensures that the attire is proper and agreeable to behold. The Sister Deputy removes any decorations or ornaments causing discomfort and places a blue ribbon upon her, presenting the hands of the Initiate to the V.I.M. as a sign of submission. She observes the candidate in question to confirm her readiness to proceed. The V.I.M. instructs the Deputy to prepare the Initiate for admission.

The Sister then examines the hands of the Initiate, removes any grandeur of rings, and places her beside the blue ribbon that awaits her. A distinct veil is placed upon her, with the remark that the innocent are always entitled to justice. This symbolizes the truth she must later demonstrate and reveals the right angles that Freemasons hold under the guidance of truth. She uncovers the left shoulder and blindfolds her with a white handkerchief, requiring her to develop a firm and blind faith, and to act accordingly.

She is then introduced into the ceremony by five knocks.
