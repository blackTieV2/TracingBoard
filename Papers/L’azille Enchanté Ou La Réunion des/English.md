## The Enchanted Azille or The Reunion of the Two Sexes Apprentice Grade 

### Chamber of Reflection

This chamber must be adorned in white with a table in the middle, covered with a white cloth, two armchairs, one in front of the table and the other beside it. The Initiate is seated within without a chair, facing the table. The second armchair is used on days when the illustrious Master or Warden is present there. The table must include a vanity mirror and a writing desk with a single lit lamp in the said chamber.
