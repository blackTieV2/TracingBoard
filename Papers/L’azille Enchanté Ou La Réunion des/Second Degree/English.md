## Grade of Companionship
________________________________________
Preparation of the Recipient
The Sister Substitute of the Worshipful Master leads the Recipient into the Chamber of Reflection, where she is confined. After she has remained there for some time, the Sister Warder enters and says to her:
"My Sister, what do I see? How degraded you are! What can I do for you?"
The Recipient responds:
"My Sister, give me your assistance."
The Sister Warder continues:
"My Sister, place your left knee, your left hand, and your right foot on the ground, and place your right hand upon the Book of the Gospel."
She then removes the blindfold and says to her:
"My Sister, continue to place all your trust in the Sister Substitute, who will guide you."

Decoration of the Lodge
The tracing board of the lodge is divided into six parts by two vertical and one horizontal line and contains six tableaux. These tableaux represent:
1.	At the top left, the Tower of Babel.
2.	In the center at the top, the Ark of Noah.
3.	At the top right, the destruction of the Tower of Babel and the sacrifice of Abraham.
4.	At the bottom left, Lot's wife turned into a pillar of salt.
5.	At the bottom center, the sacrifice of Abraham and the ladder of Jacob.
6.	At the bottom right, Joseph in the cistern.
Below these scenes, the quadrangular table contains two globes, one terrestrial and one celestial, accompanied by an inscription, with a compass and square at its center.
On this same table are placed:
•	The compass, square, and plumb-line,
•	The two candlesticks,
•	The apron of the grade, and other tools required for the ceremonial.
The entire lodge is illuminated with natural light from above and candles placed in strategic locations, all symbolizing spiritual and divine enlightenment.
The floor of the lodge is adorned with a carpet where various emblems and symbols are arranged. Among these, the ladder with its ascending steps, symbolizing the elevation of the spirit and the path to wisdom, occupies a prominent position in the center.
The lodge is surrounded by dark drapery symbolizing the mysteries that it contains, while the ceiling is adorned with stars and other celestial representations to signify the grand scope of the divine order.

Reception
The Lodge opens as usual, and as soon as it is opened, the Brother Master of Ceremonies strikes two knocks at the door of the Lodge, which the Grand Inspector hears, and after the usual formalities, the Grand Inspector orders the Brother Master of Ceremonies to introduce the Recipient.
The Grand Inspector then says to the Recipient:
"Madam, do you wish to submit to the severe tests that all Apprentices must undergo to become a Companion?"
The Recipient responds affirmatively.
The Grand Inspector continues:
"Madam, your response pleases me, but first, let me test your confidence and perfect obedience for your guide."
The Grand Inspector then gives the command to the Brother Master of Ceremonies to cover the eyes of the Recipient and to bind her left leg and right arm, with one shoe removed, leaving the other foot bare. Once done, the Brother Master of Ceremonies announces to the Grand Inspector that everything is ready for the tests.
The Grand Inspector orders the Brother Master of Ceremonies to lead the Recipient on the journey. The latter then acts as the guide for the Recipient, presenting her as a Traveler. He announces that they are embarking on the journey.

Discourse
"My dear Sister, nothing is more pleasant for us than to see you among us in this place consecrated to the cultivation of virtue. You were summoned here to join our Society, and you are admitted to the sublime Grade of Companionship, after having satisfied the requirements and received the necessary instructions.
You are now in the presence of the Great Light that dispels the shadows of ignorance and reveals the mysteries of our practices, which aim to perfect the heart and the mind.
This sublime degree we confer upon you brings both honor and responsibility. It obliges you to guide your life by the principles of virtue and love for humanity, to which we are dedicated as Sisters and Brothers.
We hope that the lessons and truths of this grade will enlighten you, that you may radiate them around you and remain worthy of the esteem and affection of your Sisters and Brothers."
"The secrets that we reveal to you today are precious and must remain sacred to you. They form the core of our commitment to truth and enlightenment. You will notice the symbols around you, representing wisdom, strength, and beauty. These are the supports of our Lodge and the virtues we aim to embody.
The ladder you see before you is the ladder of Jacob, which symbolizes the ascent toward perfection. The levels represent faith, hope, and charity. May you always climb it with determination and humility.
The temple represents the edifice of your soul. Just as the ancient builders sought to raise their structures to the glory of the Great Architect of the Universe, you are called to construct within yourself a temple worthy of divine grace.
Remember always that this grade is not an end but a new beginning. By advancing in knowledge and virtue, you prepare yourself for even greater truths that await you in the higher grades."
"Sister, the honor we bestow upon you today is not merely ceremonial. It is a reminder of your duties to your Sisters and Brothers, to humanity, and to the divine.
We trust that you will faithfully fulfill the obligations of this grade, that you will guard the secrets entrusted to you with integrity, and that you will labor tirelessly to spread the light of our Order.
Let us now unite in a solemn wish: may your journey within our Lodge be fruitful, and may the principles of this grade inspire you always. Sister, welcome to the Circle of Companions. May your path be illuminated by wisdom, guided by strength, and beautified by love."

Instruction:
The approval of death, my dear sisters, represents the state of man after his fall; the imprudence of our sister has taught you that every person, in the depths of their soul, is a slave to their passions. This sign should signify for you the steadfastness we grant you by instructing you in the realm of felicity, to which you must compare the divine light you have seen among the people you admire. And, as we use it to show you our master tables and the nourishment we find there, it must be regarded as a support and defense of masonry.

Sign
The sign is given by carrying the right hand to the mouth as if to close it, then beating the chest with the same hand five times successively, while observing a humble and sorrowful expression.
One responds to this sign by replicating it and presenting the same gestures. The word of recognition associated with this is "Bébé," which signifies innocence.
The sign also symbolizes the humility of the recipient in acknowledging their imperfections. After performing the sign, the person must pronounce the word "Repentance," meaning "I have sinned," in a tone filled with sorrow and sincerity.
After this, the recipient is led to the seat of reflection where they are reminded of the importance of moral virtue. Each Brother and Sister in the Lodge is then invited to extend their right hand as a gesture of welcoming and inclusion.
The final step of this ritual is a collective affirmation. The Lodge gathers in a circle, with the recipient standing at the center. Each member repeats the sign in unison, symbolizing unity and shared purpose.
The Grand Instructor concludes by stating: "This sign is a reminder of our commitment to fraternity and the humility we must carry in our hearts."

"Questions"
1.	Question: What is your grade?
o	Response: Companion.
2.	Question: Who gave you your grade?
o	Response: The Master of the Lodge.
3.	Question: What is the emblem of your grade?
o	Response: The square and the compass.
4.	Question: What does the emblem signify?
o	Response: Rectitude in judgment and harmony in conduct.
5.	Question: Where is the place of a Companion?
o	Response: Among the columns of the Lodge.
6.	Question: What is the state of a Companion’s mind?
o	Response: Always in search of truth.
7.	Question: How is truth represented?
o	Response: By the light of knowledge, illuminating the darkness of ignorance.
8.	Question: What is the path of truth for a Companion?
o	Response: To practice virtue and pursue knowledge.
9.	Question: What does the Word signify?
o	Response: The unity and concord established among Masons.
10.	Question: What is the Companion’s Word?
o	Response: "Babel."
11.	Question: What does it symbolize?
o	Response: The search for understanding and the effort to overcome confusion.
12.	Question: What is the ultimate goal of a Companion?
o	Response: To achieve enlightenment and to act with justice and wisdom.
13.	Question: How does one attain the state of enlightenment?
o	Response: Through perseverance, moral rectitude, and dedication to the greater good.
14.	Question: What age are you as a Companion?
o	Response: Seven years.
15.	Final Remarks: The ceremony concludes with the Master delivering closing instructions, reminding the Companion of their obligations to the Lodge and the principles of unity, truth, and harmony. The Lodge is then closed with a period of solemn silence.
